Our Story

SANGANAK was founded with a simple mission: to make quality education accessible to every student. We believe that with the right guidance, any student can achieve their academic goals.

Starting with a passion for teaching, SANGANAK has grown to serve hundreds of students across multiple subjects and levels. Our team of expert tutors brings years of classroom experience and genuine care for student success.
