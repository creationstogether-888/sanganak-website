Frequently Asked Questions

Is the first session really free?

Yes! Your first 20-minute session is completely free. We use this time to understand your needs and create a personalized learning plan.

How long are sessions?

Standard sessions are 1 hour. We can arrange longer or shorter sessions based on your needs.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes! We offer flexible scheduling. You can reschedule with 24 hours notice.

Do you offer online tutoring?

Yes! We offer both online and in-person sessions. Choose what works best for you.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel anytime, no long-term contracts. Cancellations within 48 hours of a session may incur a charge.

How do I pay?

We accept card payments (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and bank transfers. Payment is due on or before your first session.
